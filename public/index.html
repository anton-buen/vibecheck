Why "vibecheck"? Philosophical Grounding

The Everyday Problem:

Bound by propriety (hiya) and passive resiliency, students routinely default to saying "Okay lang" ("I am fine") in noisy group chats—suffering in isolation while projecting a fabricated image of wellness.

1.
Martin Buber
The Elements of the Interhuman

Dismantles "Seeming" (false "I'm fine" image) in favor of "Being". Reaching out to a struggling peer practices Personal Making Present and Confirmation—allowing genuine Unfolding without judgment.

2.
Filipino Ethics
Pakikipagkapwa-tao vs. Pakikisama

Moves beyond mere outward conformity (pakikisama) to true pakikipagkapwa-tao—transforming hiya and kanya-kanya syndrome into active solidarity.

3.
Vessels (1998)
Four Ethical Components

Sharpens Ethical Sensitivity (noticing distress), guides Ethical Judgment & Motivation (prioritizing human welfare), and culminates in Ethical Action (initiating an offline kumustahan).

4.
Kavanaugh, Aguas & Scheler
Reflective Morality & Freedom

Exercises Kavanaugh's Power of Distance to elevate automatic greetings from Customary Morality into a deliberate Human Act (Aguas), honoring Scheler's Spiritual Values.
