Why Vibecheck?

Breaking Past "Okay Lang"

In Filipino culture, we often default to "Okay lang" even when we're struggling. Vibecheck creates a low-friction space to share honest check-ins beyond surface pleasantries.

Pakikipagkapwa-tao

Treating others with shared inner humanity. When someone checks in at Level 1–2, we practice genuine presence: reaching out to listen, not to fix or give unsolicited advice.

Hiya & Kapwa

We balance hiya (appropriate sensitivity) with kapwa (shared identity). This tool helps overcome the shame of asking for help by making vulnerability visible and normalized.

30-Second Friction-Free Design

No accounts. No emails. Just honest daily check-ins. The 30-second timer encourages quick, authentic responses rather than overthinking.

Data & Privacy

Check-ins are stored temporarily in Upstash Redis and scoped to your room. No tracking, no selling data. You can create private rooms for trusted groups.
