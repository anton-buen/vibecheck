Why vibecheck?

The Everyday Problem

Bound by propriety (hiya) and passive resiliency, students routinely default to "Okay lang" in noisy group chats—suffering in isolation while projecting wellness.

1

Martin Buber

The Elements of the Interhuman

Dismantles "Seeming" (false "I'm fine" image) in favor of "Being". Practices Personal Making Present and Confirmation—allowing genuine Unfolding without judgment.

2

Filipino Ethics

Pakikipagkapwa-tao vs. Pakikisama

Moves beyond outward conformity (pakikisama) to true pakikipagkapwa-tao—transforming hiya into active solidarity.

3

Vessels (1998)

Four Ethical Components

Sharpens Ethical Sensitivity, guides Ethical Judgment & Motivation, and culminates in Ethical Action (initiating outreach).

4

Kavanaugh, Aguas & Scheler

Reflective Morality & Freedom

Exercises Power of Distance to elevate automatic greetings into deliberate Human Acts, honoring Spiritual Values.
